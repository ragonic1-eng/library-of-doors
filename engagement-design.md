# Engagement Design — the micro-drama engine (v0.1)

What makes Chinese short dramas (短剧) and TikTok serials impossible to put down, and how each technique is built into this game. Sources: industry coverage of the format's pacing and tropes (CNBC, ChinaFile, People's Daily, 2025), plus the craft vocabulary used by 短剧 writers.

---

## 1. The formula, named

| Craft term | Meaning | The feeling it sells |
|---|---|---|
| **虐 (nüe)** | Front-loaded suffering and humiliation of the hero | "This is unfair. I need to see it put right." |
| **爽点 (shuǎng diǎn)** | A satisfaction beat: payoff, power, recognition, revenge | Relief, pride, a rush |
| **打脸 (dǎ liǎn)** | Face-slap: the one who sneered is proven wrong in public | Vindication |
| **逆袭 (nì xí)** | The underdog's rise, usually sudden | Hope |
| **扮猪吃虎 / 隐藏身份** | Hidden power, hidden identity, revealed at the perfect moment | Anticipation of the reveal |
| **反转 (fǎn zhuǎn)** | Reversal: the situation flips in one beat | Surprise |
| **金手指** | The hero's special edge or power | Fantasy of competence |
| **钩子 (gōu zi)** | The hook: a question planted in the first three seconds and at every cut | Compulsion to continue |
| **断点** | The break point: end every episode on an unresolved beat | "One more" |

Pacing rules from the format: a hook in the first 3 seconds, a 爽点 or 反转 every 60 to 120 seconds, an episode of 1 to 3 minutes, and a 断点 at the end of every one.

## 2. What we keep and what we refuse

**Keep:** the pacing, the emotional contract (suffering → payoff), hooks, reversals, hidden identity, face-slaps, episode-ending breaks, punchy episode titles.

**Refuse:** coin paywalls at cliffhangers, fake countdowns, cliffhangers that never pay off, cheap sentiment that breaks the Gaiman register. This is a gift. The compulsion must come from caring, not from manipulation.

## 3. Why The Sandman fits the formula exactly

The Sandman's first act *is* a 短剧 plot. The most powerful being in existence is stripped, caged naked in a glass ball in a cellar, and kept there for seventy-two years by a vain English occultist and then his frightened son. Everyone who sees her thinks she is a captured curiosity. She says nothing. Then one day the circle breaks. Hidden identity, 虐, 逆袭 and 打脸 in one arc. We only have to pace it like a short drama instead of like a novel.

## 4. The engine, applied to Episode 1

Episode 1 is re-cut into five micro-episodes of 2 to 4 minutes, each with a hook, at least one 爽点 or 反转, and a 断点.

| Micro-ep | Title (short-drama style) | Opening hook (first 3 s) | 虐 | 爽点 / 反转 | 断点 (end hook) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 ◆ | **The Queen of Dreams** | Aurie on her throne, a nightmare kneeling to her. Full power, 20 seconds. | The circle takes her mid-sentence. | We *saw* her power. Every humiliation afterwards is charged. | Burgess's face through the glass: "Death. At last." |
| 1 | **The Wrong Sister** | Burgess crowing over his prize. | Stripped of helm, sand, ruby. Named "creature." | 反转: he caught the wrong Endless and does not know. The player knows. | Burgess: "Give me what I want." Conversation window opens. |
| 2 | **Seventy Years** | A soldier's open eyes in a trench. | The world breaks without her. The tools are sold off by Burgess's mistress ◆. | Burgess dies old and poor, never getting what he asked for. Small 打脸. | Alex at the glass: "If I let you go, will you hurt me?" |
| 3 | **The Son** | Alex's trembling hand near the chalk. | Decades more. Alex almost frees her in 1950 and does not. | 爽点 is the player's: she chooses whether to show him mercy or her teeth. | A guard yawns, slides down the glass, breath fogging it. |
| 4 | **Breath on Glass** | The fog forms a door. | None. The suffering is over. | **The big 爽点.** She steps out of a dream, the circle crumbles, and Alex is wheeled in screaming. The player chooses the face-slap: eternal waking, one night, or walking away. | Lucienne's lantern in the ruin: "My lady. Seventy-two years." Three locked doors. |

The 2.1 hub then plays as the "next season" trailer: three doors, a table with four empty places, and Lucienne refusing to explain. That is the 断点 for the whole first night.

## 5. The engine across the series

- **Hidden identity is the running thread.** In the mortal world nobody knows what Aurie is. Constantine, Dee, the diner, the cereal convention: each is a 扮猪吃虎 setup where the reveal is the payoff. In Hell, Lucifer's legions blocking the gate and then parting is the biggest 打脸 in Book 1.
- **Every tool reclaimed is a 金手指 unlock**: the Dreaming visibly repairs, new options appear in conversations, characters react differently. Power-ups the player can see.
- **Emotional debts pay off late.** The sleeping girl in the montage (Unity Kincaid) returns in Book 1 Episode 5 to save Rose. Hob's friendship pays off at the Wake. Alex's fate is named by the Furies. Every 虐 has a receipt.
- **Reversals are player-authored.** The game's 反转 come from her choices, so they feel earned, not watched.
- **Each book opens on its own 虐.** Richard loses his life, flat, fiancée and visibility in one evening. Coraline is ignored by her parents then offered everything by a monster. Santiago is robbed of every coin on his first day in Africa. Each closes on a 逆袭.
- **Door teases are the season finale hook.** Locked doors on the wall from day one, each with its own leaking weather, and Lucienne who knows and will not say.

## 6. Mechanics that carry it (built into the engine)

1. **`hook` on every scene.** A one-line cliffhanger overlay shown for about three seconds when a scene ends, before the next begins. Authored, in character, never a question mark aimed at the player.
2. **Micro-episode end cards** with the next title and a two-second flash of its first frame. Continue is one tap. No countdown.
3. **爽点 cadence check** in content review: no stretch longer than two minutes without a payoff, reversal or decision. Any montage over 20 seconds is cut or given a decision.
4. **Variable reward from the live AI.** Because characters improvise, the same scene never pays off the same way twice. Kindness unlocks secrets on the character cards; cruelty unlocks different ones. Secrets are the loot.
5. **Recognition beats.** Characters notice her choices by name ("You spared the boy. Why?"). Being seen is a 爽点 in itself.
6. **Return hooks for a gift, not a slot machine.** When she closes the app mid-episode, the next opening is the hook line, not a menu. The library greets her with one new sentence from Lucienne each visit. Nothing is gated, nothing expires.
7. **Titles that promise.** Episode titles are written like short-drama titles: a reversal in three words.

## 7. Episode 0, the cold open (new, ◆)

Twenty to thirty seconds before the capture, so the fall has weight. Three clips:
- **0.0a** (3P, hero visible, 10 s): the throne room of the Dreaming at full power. Aurie seated, the Corinthian kneeling, Lucienne at her shoulder with the day's report. Her hair moves in its own wind. The walls are stars.
- **0.0b** (POV, 5 s): she raises a hand and a nightmare dissolves into sand at her gesture. Pure 金手指.
- **0.0c** (3P, 10 s): mid-sentence, the floor beneath her flares with chalk-white lines. The pull. Blackness. Then the cellar. (This replaces the current 1.1a opening; 1.1b follows.)

Hook line over the cut to black: Lucienne's voice, "My lady?"
